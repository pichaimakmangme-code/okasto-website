Levels: L0 1 · L1 20 · L2 20 · L3 1 (route template, 6 routes at launch). “Built” = exists in the current build, many as sections of the one-page Home; the plan gives them their own URLs.

Status meaning

  • Built exists in the current build (some need their own URL)
  • Launch must be ready before go-live
  • Phase 2 after launch, when data / partners / backend are ready

On every page

  • Header: Routes & Prices · Fleet · Charter · Service info · Help · Book & Pay button
  • Footer: About · Contact · Booking policy · Terms · Privacy · Partners · “Also available on” partner names (grey text)
  • Language switch TH / EN / 中文
  • Floating LINE / WhatsApp for questions only — never for booking or payment
  • Mobile: sticky “Book & Pay” bar

URL and SEO rules

  • One URL per language: /en/… · /th/… · /zh/… with hreflang + x-default
  • Short English slugs, e.g. /en/transfer/bkk-to-pattaya
  • Booking, manage, draft legal and admin pages: noindex
  • Schema: Organization, FAQPage on FAQ/landing pages; XML sitemap + robots.txt
  • Every landing page has its own prefilled booking widget

Payment

  • Online only via Beam (card · PromptPay · mobile banking · e-wallet as enabled). No cash, no manual transfer.

4 · Journeys

User journeys เส้นทางผู้ใช้หลัก

J1 Book & Pay (main path)
จองและชำระเงิน
Home / landing page→Search & quote→Choose vehicle→Details & review→Pay with Beam→Confirmation + email
If payment fails → Payment failed · retry (keeps trip details)
J2 Manage booking
จัดการการจอง
Confirmation email→Manage booking (ref + email)→View · change · cancel→Policy check: free ≥24 h→Refund via Beam
Launch: change/cancel request by email · Phase 2: full self-service
J3 Search engine → booking
จาก Google สู่การจอง
Google: 'BKK to Pattaya transfer'→Route / airport page→Prefilled widget→Choose vehicle→… Pay → Confirmation
SEO pages carry their own prefilled widget, so the funnel stays 4 clicks
J4 Question before booking
มีคำถามก่อนจอง
Help centre / FAQ→LINE / WhatsApp (questions only)→Back to Book & Pay
Chat answers questions; bookings and payments always go through Book & Pay
J5 Arrival day
วันเดินทาง
Meeting points guide→Driver with name sign→Contact if needed
Flight tracking + free waiting on arrivals
J6 Partner / supply (Phase 2)
พาร์ทเนอร์ (เฟส 2)
Partners page→Enquiry form → email→Drive with OKASTO → apply
Forms send email only at first; no partner portal yet

Funnel target: price on the first screen, payment in 4 steps (search → vehicle → details → pay). Free cancellation ≥24 h shown at vehicle choice and review.

6 · Roadmap

Assumptions and open questions สมมติฐานและเรื่องที่ต้องยืนยัน

Assumptions

  1. Home sections (Routes & Prices, Fleet, Luggage, Charter, Service info, FAQ) are proposed as their own URLs for SEO; the one-page Home keeps short teasers.
  2. U-Tapao (UTP) and Hua Hin landing pages are Phase 2; prices already exist for UTP.
  3. Manage booking is “lite” at launch (lookup + change/cancel request by email) because there is no booking backend yet.
  4. Route pages are generated from prices.json; travel times and distances are TBC and must not be guessed.
  5. Partner / B2B and driver-recruitment pages are proposals; commission, net rates and API need CEO decision.
  6. Corporate customers also pay online via Beam; tax-invoice process TBC.
  7. Reviews and safety pages appear only with real, verifiable content.
  8. Domain and final URL language prefix are TBC.

Decisions needed from the CEO

  1. Approve launch scope (Phase 0 + Phase 1).
  2. Confirm the top 6 launch routes.
  3. Go / no-go for Partners, Corporate, Hotels and Drive-with-us pages.
  4. Permission to show partner logos (until then: grey text).
  5. Refund timing via Beam to state on the policy page.
  6. Lawyer review date for Terms & Privacy.

Always true on the site

  • Payment online via Beam only — no cash, no bank-transfer details.
  • LINE / WhatsApp for questions only; booking through Book & Pay.
  • Only confirmed facts: founded 19 Jan 2024 · 200+ vehicles · partner list.
  • No secret keys in front-end code.
