OKASTO price editor · internal

⚠ Internal tool, NOT for public use. There is no password: do not link to this page. After editing, download prices.json and prices-data.js, then upload them to data/prices.json and js/prices-data.js on your host. This page never sends data anywhere. Your draft is kept only in this browser.

Settings

Site price = rate × (1 + markup/100), rounded to the nearest "round to". Leave markup at 0 to show the rates exactly as entered.

Transfer prices (THB per vehicle, one-way)

Pick an origin. Leave a box blank = that vehicle is NOT offered on that route. Prices are the base rates. The site applies the markup.

or a new place:

Charter (THB per hour, per vehicle)

Blank = not offered (e.g. Electric SUV).

Place names (shown on the site in TH / EN / ZH)

"Area" groups places so the site does not offer duplicate reverse routes, e.g. all Pattaya zones are area PATTAYA. Leave it blank if unsure.

Vehicles

Vehicle names and models are translated in js/i18n.js. Here you can edit capacity only. The column order is fixed.
